Enterprise AI rarely eliminates work as cleanly as the business case suggests. It relocates that work into verification, quality assurance, exception handling, audit, and the [continuous human judgment](/frameworks/power-user-trap/) required to make automated output usable.

## AI relocates human labor. Vendors call it autonomous.

If you want to evaluate different options, it helps to know a few details:

- The labor does not disappear. 
- It moves.
- It moves from drafting to verification.
- From production to correction.
- From search to source validation.
- From workflow execution to exception handling.
- From decision support to liability review.
- From “doing the work” to proving the machine did not break the work.

This is [the part the automation story prefers not to tell.](/blog/ai-investment-scaling-human-capacity/) 

## Lozen Advisory can help you identify where the work went.

The vendor pitch depends on treating human judgment as residual, occasional, or supervisory. In practice, human beings remain the load-bearing infrastructure that makes AI usable, safe, defensible, and institutionally acceptable.

[The work has not vanished.](/blog/menopause-at-work-unquantified-data-gap/)

It has been relocated into the people responsible for checking, correcting, escalating, contextualizing, validating, and owning the output.

That is not autonomy.

That is [labor displacement inside the workflow.](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/services/consulting/articles/defensive-ux-responsible-gen-ai-framework.html)
