Lozen Advisory provides market intelligence on Algorithmic Accountability — the evidence gaps that leave boards and CFOs unable to prove who authorized an AI-assisted decision, who reviewed it, and who was accountable when something went wrong.

The core failure is a measurement problem, not a technology problem. Legacy governance systems were built to see declared processes, formal roles, and documented approvals. A framework can exist, an adoption metric can look strong, and a policy can be filed — while the organization still cannot answer the only question that matters afterward: whose authority, review, and evidence remained intact while the decision was made. A board relying on that framework is relying on evidence that was never designed to answer the question it is now being asked.

Lozen Advisory has identified the same structural failure across AI and workforce systems. When measurement depends on formal disclosure, documented use, or visible participation, institutions systematically undercount risks that people have rational reasons not to disclose. In AI governance, that blind spot appears as undocumented human review, unclear sign-off authority, hidden verification labor, and AI-assisted work entering the corporate record without a defensible accountability chain.

Market Intelligence helps boards, CFOs, and their advisors see these gaps clearly before an incident, a proxy challenge, an insurance claim, or a director's oversight duty forces the question. The work identifies what current governance evidence is failing to capture, why a documented process is not the same as a documented accountability chain, and where leadership should focus before the gap becomes a board, legal, or reputational exposure.

Deliverables may include executive briefings, market scans, governance-pattern analysis, comparative institutional findings, and decision-ready recommendations tailored to the client's oversight responsibilities.

Engagements may be commissioned as a focused research mandate or structured as recurring intelligence support for organizations tracking changes in AI accountability, governance evidence, insurance exposure, and institutional risk.

Market Intelligence interprets the external market for the client. It is distinct from the Advisory Engagement, which applies Lozen Advisory's framework inside the client's own organization; the Governance Readiness Briefing, which diagnoses a defined internal question; and Crisis, Reputation, and Algorithmic Accountability Risk support, which responds to an already activated exposure.
